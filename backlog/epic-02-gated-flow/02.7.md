### 02.7 — Lead capture, storage & duplicate detection

**What & why.** The write path — the point at which a visitor becomes an entry on the list, which is the
only durable output this entire project produces. Everything else in the epic is the experience around
this one moment.

It also owns **duplicate detection**: because the experience is stateless, the same person can reach the
form more than once, so the server decides on **email** whether this is a new signup or a return visit,
and tells scene 4 which ending to play ([02.6](02.6.md)).

This is the epic's **highest-risk story** and should be treated as such. A lead that silently fails to
save is invisible, unrecoverable, and indistinguishable from a lead that never came — there is no
downstream check that would ever catch it. Every other story in this epic can be wrong and then fixed;
this one can be wrong and never noticed.

**Value archetype.** Acquisition — this *is* the asset. Without this story the other seven produce
nothing but a nice animation.

**Surface / UX fit.** No surface of its own — it is the capability behind scene 3's submit and scene 4's
branch. Registered in [`screens-map.md`](../../product/screens-map.md) under "not a product surface".

**Prioritization.** Blocks 02.6's second ending and is what makes the epic's definition of done provable.
Cannot close before [01.2](../epic-01-foundation/01.2.md) (the data layer) and
[01.4](../epic-01-foundation/01.4.md) (the seam) are real — 02.5 can be built against a stub, but this
story is where the stub has to become the actual store.

**Edge cases — resolved.**
- **Known email.** No second row. The existing entry is preserved and scene 4 is told to play the
  already-listed ending.
- **Email casing and whitespace.** Normalized before the duplicate check, or the same person trivially
  creates two entries.
- **Concurrent duplicate submissions.** The uniqueness rule holds at the store, not only in application
  logic — a double-submit race must not be able to create two rows.
- **Write failure.** Surfaced to the visitor rather than swallowed ([02.5](02.5.md) keeps their input).
  A silent failure is the one outcome this story exists to prevent.
- **Personal data at rest.** Name, email, and phone are personal data — they are not to be logged in
  plaintext, echoed into error messages, or exposed to the client beyond what the visitor typed.
- **Retrieval.** No admin surface in this epic (owner's call), so the list has to be retrievable and
  exportable by the owner directly from the store. "Captured" is not the same as "reachable."

**Edge cases — deferred.**
- **Rate limiting / flood protection.** Considered, deferred as a build blocker but tracked as a
  **launch blocker** — an open capture endpoint can be scripted to fill the list with garbage. Resolve
  in this story's `tech-planning` pass.
- Considered, deferred — capture provenance (which channel or campaign a signup came from). The owner
  chose one shared code, which forecloses attribution by design; revisit only if multiple codes ever
  land.
- Considered, deferred — retention policy for collected data. Downstream of
  [Q-01](../../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md).

**Acceptance criteria (product-level).**
- A completed form produces exactly one retrievable entry containing name, email, and phone.
- Submitting the same email again produces no second entry and reports "already listed" to the flow.
- Casing and whitespace differences in an email do not create a duplicate.
- Two simultaneous submissions of the same email cannot both create an entry.
- A failed write is reported to the visitor, never silently dropped.
- The owner can retrieve and export the full list without an admin surface.
- No personal data appears in logs or error output.
