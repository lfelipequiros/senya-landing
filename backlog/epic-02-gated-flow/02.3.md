### 02.3 — Scene 1 — opening

**What & why.** The first thing a visitor sees, and the only thing standing between an Instagram tap and
a bounce. It has two jobs: assert that this is a brand worth paying attention to, and make the visitor
scroll. Nothing else — no navigation, no explanation, no copy block.

At this epic's fidelity the animation is a **placeholder with real timing**: generic marks and type, but
the actual pacing, entrance choreography, and scroll cue the real thing will inherit. [Epic 03](../epic-03-brand-motion/README.md)
swaps the identity into the choreography this story fixes.

**Value archetype.** Brand perception — this scene produces no capture and exists purely to set tone.
Judged on whether it earns the scroll, not on signups.

**Surface / UX fit.** State 1 of the single surface; the URL-reachable entry point. Consumes 02.2's
motion primitives; adds none of its own.

**Prioritization.** After 02.1/02.2. Ahead of the gate and form because it sets the pacing reference the
rest of the run is timed against — how long this scene holds decides what the gate's arrival should feel
like.

**Edge cases — resolved.**
- **Visitor scrolls immediately, before the animation finishes.** The scroll wins. The opening must never
  trap someone who is already convinced — the animation yields to intent rather than blocking on
  completion.
- **Every visit replays it in full.** Deliberate ([PDR-002](../../product/decisions.md)) — accepted cost
  of the stateless stance, and the reason this scene must stay short.
- **No scroll affordance discovered.** A visitor who doesn't realize scrolling is the verb is a total
  loss, so an explicit scroll cue is required — not optional polish.
- **Reduced motion.** Resolves to 02.2's calmer path; the scroll cue must still be present.
- **Arriving on desktop.** Supported, not the design reference. Must not look broken.

**Edge cases — deferred.**
- Considered, deferred — audio or sound design on the opening. Real brand potential, but it needs an
  unmute affordance and a policy of its own; out of scope until the identity exists.
- Considered, deferred — a first-frame poster/preload treatment for slow connections. Revisit when the
  real assets land in Epic 03.

**Acceptance criteria (product-level).**
- The opening plays on load with placeholder marks and finished timing.
- A visible cue communicates that scrolling continues the experience.
- Scrolling during the animation advances immediately rather than waiting for it to end.
- The scene holds up on a phone and does not break on desktop.
- Under reduced motion the scene still presents and still cues the scroll.
