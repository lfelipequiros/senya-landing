### 02.6 — Scene 4 — confirmation & the already-listed ending

**What & why.** The last thing a visitor sees, and the only payoff they get for clearing a gate and
handing over their phone number. It has to confirm unambiguously that they are in — an ambiguous ending
generates "did it work?" resubmissions and erodes trust in the brand at the exact moment it was just
earned.

There are **two endings**, not one. Because the experience keeps no client-side memory
([PDR-002](../../product/decisions.md)), the same person can legitimately reach the form twice; when the
email is already on the list, they get a distinct **"you're already on the list"** ending rather than a
silent duplicate or a generic success. The owner chose honesty here over the invisible-dedupe option:
telling someone they're already in is a warmer moment than pretending to add them again, and it removes
any doubt about whether the first attempt worked.

**Value archetype.** Brand perception — the ending converts a completed transaction into a feeling.
Secondarily Acquisition, since a confident ending is what stops anxious resubmissions.

**Surface / UX fit.** State 4 of the single surface. Both endings are states of scene 4, not separate
surfaces. Establishes the two-ending convention that [PDR-002](../../product/decisions.md) records as
the visible consequence of statelessness.

**Prioritization.** Depends on 02.5 and on 02.7's duplicate detection — the second ending cannot be
built before the server can say whether an email is known.

**Edge cases — resolved.**
- **New email.** The standard confirmation ending.
- **Email already on the list.** The distinct already-listed ending — warm, not an error, and explicitly
  not styled like the gate's failure state.
- **Same person, different email.** Treated as a new signup. Accepted: identity is the email, and no
  cross-field matching is attempted.
- **What happens after the ending.** The run is over. No navigation onward — there is nowhere else to
  go, and inventing somewhere would dilute the close.
- **Reload on the confirmation.** Returns to scene 1 and replays, like everywhere else. A visitor who
  does this and re-submits reaches the already-listed ending, which is exactly why it exists.
- **Reduced motion.** Both endings resolve to 02.2's calmer path and remain clearly distinguishable from
  each other.

**Edge cases — deferred.**
- Considered, deferred — a share/refer affordance on the confirmation ("send this to a friend"). Real
  acquisition potential and a natural fit for a code-gated drop, but it needs a referral story of its
  own. Candidate for `08-adhoc`.
- Considered, deferred — a confirmation email. Depends on Q-01's consent outcome and on having any
  sending infrastructure at all.

**Acceptance criteria (product-level).**
- A successful new signup lands on a confirmation that leaves no doubt the visitor is on the list.
- A repeat email lands on a visibly different already-listed ending that reads as reassurance, not error.
- Neither ending resembles the gate's glitch failure state.
- Neither ending offers onward navigation.
- Both endings are distinguishable under reduced motion.
