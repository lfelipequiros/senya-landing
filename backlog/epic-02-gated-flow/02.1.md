### 02.1 — Scene engine & flow state machine

**What & why.** The visitor moves through four scenes in one unbroken run, and the rules of that
movement are the product. This story builds the state machine that owns which scene is live, what
advances it, and what is reachable — so every later scene story plugs into a decided structure instead
of inventing its own navigation.

The transitions are deliberately not uniform: **scroll** carries the visitor from scene 1 to scene 2,
but once the gate is cleared, **acceptance** drives 3 and 4 in place. Scroll stops being the verb at the
locked door, because a visitor must never be able to scroll past a gate they haven't opened.

**Value archetype.** Enabler — no direct value; every other story in this epic is built on it.

**Surface / UX fit.** Establishes the project's only surface (see
[`screens-map.md`](../../product/screens-map.md)). Registers the four scenes as **states of one
surface**, not four routes — only scenes 1–2 are URL-reachable; 3 and 4 exist only as the result of a
successful gate, and are unreachable by address ([PDR-002](../../product/decisions.md)).

**Prioritization.** First story in the epic — 02.3 through 02.6 are all blocked on it. Built before any
individual scene precisely so scene work can't quietly fork the navigation model.

**Edge cases — resolved.**
- **Deep-linking into a gated scene.** Not possible; 3 and 4 have no address. A visitor who tries lands
  at scene 1.
- **Browser back/refresh at any point.** Returns to scene 1 and replays. No memory, by design
  ([PDR-002](../../product/decisions.md)).
- **Scroll position on re-entry.** Always the top. The experience has exactly one starting point.
- **Scroll during a locked scene.** Bounded — the visitor cannot scroll past the gate.

**Edge cases — deferred.**
- Considered, deferred — a "skip the intro" affordance. It contradicts the owner's stateless full-replay
  call; revisit only if drop-off at scene 1 proves to be real.

**Acceptance criteria (product-level).**
- A visitor can move from scene 1 to scene 2 by scrolling, and cannot scroll beyond the gate.
- Clearing the gate advances to scene 3 in place, without a scroll or a page change.
- Submitting advances to scene 4 in place.
- No URL reaches scene 3 or 4 directly; attempting it starts the experience from scene 1.
- A refresh at any point restarts the full run from scene 1.
