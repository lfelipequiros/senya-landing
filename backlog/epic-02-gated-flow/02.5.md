### 02.5 — Scene 3 — the lead form

**What & why.** The only scene that produces the asset. An invited visitor gives **name, email, and
phone/WhatsApp**, and every additional second of friction here costs signups that the gate has already
worked hard to qualify. Three fields is the owner's chosen balance: enough to address someone personally
and reach them on the channel that actually matters for a clothing drop, and little enough to finish on
a phone in under a minute.

Validation has to hold an uncomfortable line — it must catch a genuinely unusable address or number
(a lead you can't reach isn't a lead), while never blocking a real person over formatting. Given
[PDR-001](../../product/decisions.md)'s terminal failure stance next door, this scene is explicitly the
*opposite*: the gate is unforgiving, the form is forgiving. Nothing a visitor types here should ever
end their run.

**Value archetype.** Acquisition — this story is the capture. It is the highest-leverage scene in the
epic and the one where friction is most expensive.

**Surface / UX fit.** State 3 of the single surface, reachable only by clearing the gate. Establishes
the forgiving-input convention that [PDR-001](../../product/decisions.md) carves out as the deliberate
counterweight to the gate's severity.

**Prioritization.** After the gate exists. This is where the epic's value is realized, so it gets the
most attention to friction — field order, keyboard types, and error placement are product decisions
here, not implementation details.

**Edge cases — resolved.**
- **Invalid email or phone.** Corrected inline, in place, non-destructively. Never a glitch, never a
  reset — [PDR-001](../../product/decisions.md) explicitly scopes the terminal treatment to the gate
  only.
- **Mobile keyboards.** Each field requests the right keyboard (email, telephone) — on a mobile-first
  build this is a completion-rate lever, not a nicety.
- **International phone numbers.** Accepted with country context rather than assuming one country; a
  number that can't be dialed is worthless.
- **Partial completion / abandonment.** Nothing is captured. No partial record and no resume, consistent
  with the stateless stance.
- **Double-tap on submit.** Cannot produce two submissions.
- **Submission failure (network/server).** The visitor must be told, and their typed values must survive
  so they can retry — losing someone's input after they cleared a gate would be the worst failure in the
  product.
- **Reduced motion.** Field entrances resolve to 02.2's calmer path; error messages never rely on motion.

**Edge cases — deferred.**
- **Consent + privacy statement.** Blocked — see **[OPEN-QUESTIONS.md Q-01](../../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)**.
  Personal data (name, email, phone) needs a consent affordance and a privacy statement before this
  reaches real traffic. Does not block building the scene; **does block launching it**.
- Considered, deferred — email verification / double opt-in. Addresses are trusted as typed in the first
  build.
- Considered, deferred — asking for size, city, or Instagram handle. Weighed during the interview and cut
  to protect completion rate; revisit only if the list proves too thin to act on.

**Acceptance criteria (product-level).**
- The form appears in place after the gate is cleared, asking for name, email, and phone/WhatsApp.
- Each field brings up the appropriate mobile keyboard.
- Invalid input is corrected inline; no input a visitor can type ends their run.
- Submitting twice in quick succession produces one signup.
- A failed submission tells the visitor and preserves everything they typed.
- The form is completable one-handed on a phone.
